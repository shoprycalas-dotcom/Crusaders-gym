About the Gym

FORGED IN THE RING

Crusaders Gym is Peterborough's home for real boxing. Whether you're stepping through the ropes for the first time or preparing for competition, this is where fighters are made.

Led by Giorgio Mundo, one of the most respected boxing coaches in the city, the gym offers world-class instruction rooted in decades of experience. Giorgio has trained amateurs, professionals, and everyone in between — from kids finding their confidence to seasoned fighters chasing titles.

Our facility features a full-size boxing ring, heavy bags, speed bags, and a complete strength and conditioning area. More than equipment, we offer a community built on respect, hard work, and the belief that boxing transforms lives.

Our Programs

WHAT WE OFFER

Structured programs designed to push you forward, no matter where you start.

Beginner Boxing

Learn the fundamentals of boxing — footwork, stance, basic combinations, and defence. No experience needed. Just bring your willingness to work.

Advanced Sparring

For experienced fighters looking to sharpen their skills. Controlled sparring sessions with technical feedback from Coach Mundo and his team.

Fitness Boxing

High-intensity boxing workouts designed for fitness. Burn calories, build lean muscle, and relieve stress — all without taking a single punch.

Youth Programs

Dedicated sessions for young people. We build confidence, discipline, and respect alongside boxing technique. A positive environment for kids to thrive.

1-to-1 Coaching

Personal training sessions with Giorgio Mundo. Tailored to your goals — whether that's getting fight-ready, improving technique, or building fitness.

Competition Prep

Full fight camp preparation for amateur and professional bouts. Strategy, conditioning, sparring, and mental preparation to step in the ring at your best.
